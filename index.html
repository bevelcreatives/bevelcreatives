About us

A studio built to rescue
games worth saving.

Bevel Creatives is a Game Development & Recovery Studio from Nepal, founded in 2024 by Bibidh Gautam and Kaustuv Kumar Batajoo, built to rejuvenate the struggling games of passionate developers.

Our team dives deep into your game's mechanics, player behavior, and monetization strategy to find exactly where things stalled, then executes the fix.

We recruited a team of young, sharp minds and trained each one in a single specialized field: technically precise, and uniquely tuned to how modern players, especially teenage audiences, actually play.

Working with us

From first contact to full partnership.

The practical details: how we protect your project, how we get paid, and how the timeline actually looks.

01

NDA & confidentiality

Before we look at a single number, we sign an NDA. Your metrics, source files, and roadmap stay strictly between us.

02

Audit & proposal

We run a free audit against your retention, monetization, and engagement data, then send back a written proposal covering what we'd change, why, and what it's worth to your game.

03

Contract & compensation

Terms are documented in a straightforward revenue-share contract with no upfront fees. Larger studios can opt for a hybrid model (a small retainer plus a reduced share); for select titles, we'll put forward a straight acquisition offer instead.

04

Collaboration timeline

Most engagements open with a 1 to 2 week audit, move into a 4 to 6 week rollout of the agreed upgrades, then settle into ongoing live-ops for as long as the partnership keeps paying off.

05

Growth & acquisitions

As results compound, we revisit terms together: extend the partnership, renegotiate the split, or, for the right game, make an outright acquisition offer.
